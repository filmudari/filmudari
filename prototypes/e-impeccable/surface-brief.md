## Direction contract

THESIS: The campaign is ten film cans waiting to be filled. The page is one galvanized can lid seen from above, sealed with camera tape, and every working part is written on tape. It refuses the dark screening room, the catalogue, the poster and the sketchbook, and the generic split hero with a card row.

OWN-WORLD: Galvanized steel grey ground pressed with concentric lid rings; white camera tape strips carrying set type; marker-black ink; fluoro chartreuse tape as the only action and money colour; each film's own tape colour on its can. Archivo (condensed to wide) for all set type, Big Shoulders Stencil for can numbers and the figures. No cards, no hairline catalogue rules, no handwriting.

STORY: The visitor learns ten student crews need 12 000 € to shoot, sees how much tape is laid, picks a film or gives to all, and pays.
